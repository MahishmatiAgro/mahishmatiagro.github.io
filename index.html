Using dried oyster mushroom

Clean the dried mushrooms and soak them in warm water for about 20–30 minutes. When they are soft, drain, cut as needed and cook well. Use them in sabzi, pulao, soups and other dishes.

Using mushroom powder

Add a small amount to soups, gravies or other dishes while they cook. Keep it in a clean, dry, airtight container, away from moisture. Do not mix the raw powder into drinks without cooking it.

Cooking tips

  • Clean the mushrooms and cut them as the recipe needs.
  • Start with a light sauté on high heat.
  • Adjust chilli and spices to your taste.
  • Recipe times are approximate. Always cook until done.

Which areas do you deliver to?

We supply Maharajpur, Mandla and nearby areas. For other locations, especially for dried mushroom and powder, message us to check if we can send it.

How should I store fresh oyster mushrooms?

Keep them refrigerated at 8–10°C and use within 4 days of packing (best before 4 days). Wash just before cooking.

How long do dried mushrooms and powder last?

Kept airtight in a cool, dry place away from moisture and sunlight, they stay good for months. Always use a dry spoon for the powder.

How do I cook dried oyster mushrooms?

Soak them in warm water for about 20–30 minutes until soft, drain, cut as needed and cook them like fresh mushrooms in sabzi, pulao or soup.
